Our Story

About Crochet by Sha 🧶

Where every stitch is a labor of love and every piece tells a story

🧶

Hi, I'm Sha! 👋

What started as a lockdown hobby in 2020 quickly became my greatest passion. I picked up a crochet hook for the first time, watched countless tutorials, and fell completely in love with the art of creating something beautiful from a single thread.


Now, every piece you see in this shop has been made by my own hands, in my own home, with so much care and intention. Crochet by Sha isn't just a brand — it's a love letter to the craft.

The Handmade Difference

Unlike mass-produced items, every Crochet by Sha piece is entirely unique. I source high-quality, soft yarns and spend hours — sometimes days — perfecting each item. You're not just buying a product; you're getting a piece of art made specifically for you.


Custom orders are always welcome! Want a specific color, size, or design? Just send me a message on Instagram or WhatsApp.
